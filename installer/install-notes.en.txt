Tarkov Boss Monitor 1.0.11

A Windows x64 app that detects boss spawns in official local PvE raids.

- Automatically finds the Tarkov game folder during setup, with manual selection available.
- Detects the raid map and lets you choose bosses to monitor for each map.
- Can be installed while the game is running. Required logging settings are applied after the game exits.
- Cleans game logs older than seven days every day at 04:00, while protecting active logs and the newest session.
- Removes the scheduled task on uninstall and attempts to restore logging settings changed by the app.

Official local PvE only. PvP and online PvE are not supported.
The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may display a warning.
