Tarkov Boss Monitor 1.0.11 Quick Guide

1. Run TarkovBossMonitor-Setup-1.0.11-win-x64.exe.
2. Confirm the detected game folder, or select the folder containing EscapeFromTarkov.exe and Logging.config.
3. Save the bosses to monitor for each map and enter a raid. The map is detected automatically.

“Planned” only means ShallSpawn:True was found in the log. “Confirmed” means an actual boss activation record was detected.
The first zone and coordinates are not the current position. Survival status may not be available.

Setup creates a TarkovBossMonitor-LogCleanup scheduled task. It runs daily at 04:00 and removes logs older than seven days. Logs in use, the newest session, and locked files are protected.

Only official local PvE with detailed AI logging is supported. PvP and online PvE are not supported.
The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may display a warning.
